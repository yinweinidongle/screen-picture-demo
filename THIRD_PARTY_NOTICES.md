Original project code, procedural model geometry and contributor-authored documentation use the root [MIT license](LICENSE). Third-party components retain their own permissions. Maps and terrain use **public-domain Natural Earth data**. Orbital Relay combines this terrain with Solar System Scope imagery.

Orbital Relay uses the Earth Day Map, Earth Night Map and Earth Clouds by Solar System Scope under CC BY 4.0. The build converts the publisher’s JPEGs to WebP. The scene and its captures add lighting, moving clouds, an atmosphere and network graphics. The cropped titanium header is project-generated ImageGen art covered by the generated-art policy below. The screen bundles Three.js r187 under MIT and GSAP 3.15.1 under the GSAP Standard License. Credits and upstream notices travel with both the web and offline distributions. Natural Earth's [primary terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) explicitly allow commercial use, modification and redistribution. The retained [permission record](docs/licensing/natural-earth-PUBLIC-DOMAIN.txt), [pinned source archives](config/asset-sources.json) and [runtime inventory](assets/manifest.json) provide a reproducible provenance chain. Normal builds use committed derivatives without contacting a map service. See [regeneration instructions](docs/assets.md).

Upstream boundary conventions and small-scale generalization can differ from the previous provider. The grouping of regions preserves the demo UI; no old coordinates or raster pixels are inputs to the replacement pipeline. Maps and statistics are illustrative. Reference images, original CAD and third-party trademarks are not licensed by this repository. Earlier Git history retains its historical permission status; the clearance described here concerns the current map/texture files and newly rebuilt downloads.

Wangshu Station — asset provenance

The project owner supplied the wangshu-three station model, midnight environment,
baked WS 700 animation, material flags, cinematic paths, scan shader and
synthesized arrival soundtrack for this gallery and requested publication on GitHub.
The sky and platform signs are part of that supplied project. The GLB in this
distribution replaces the original lunar photograph with a procedurally drawn
surface. See scripts/prepare_wangshu_station.py. The platform still and gallery
thumbnail are captures of the adapted scene. The supplied media are separate
from the repository's code-only MIT grant; no broader media license is asserted.

The transparent lunar-rail fascia was created with OpenAI ImageGen for this
dashboard and follows the separate generated-art policy in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

Three.js and the bundled meshoptimizer decoder have separate MIT notices.

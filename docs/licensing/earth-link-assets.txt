Orbital Relay / 全球链路监测

Earth Day Map, Earth Night Map and Earth Clouds by Solar System Scope.
Publisher and originals: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The repository includes the publisher's 2K JPEGs. The build encodes them as WebP.
The scene applies lighting, cloud drift, atmosphere, and illustrative network overlays.
The gallery preview and the static globe include these adaptations.

The relief normal map and locator silhouettes derive from public-domain Natural Earth data.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The blank titanium fascia is project artwork generated with OpenAI ImageGen and
cropped and resized for the header. It follows the repository's generated-art policy.
